# Investor DB: public sources, size of the reachable universe, failure modes

Evidence base for the PLAN section of the test task "Reliable database of investors in companies".
Prepared 2026-10-11. All "accessed" dates are 2026-10-11 unless stated otherwise.

**How the numbers were produced.** Three kinds of figures appear below:

- **[computed]**: I counted them myself from a free official bulk file downloaded on 2026-10-11, such as the SEC adviser file, SEC Form D data sets, the ESMA register Solr endpoint, the GLEIF API, the EIF PDF and the EBAN PDF. Re-running the same download gives the same count.
- **[cited]**: the figure is quoted from a public report or page. The URL and the publication date are given where known.
- **[estimate]**: my own reasoned estimate. The arithmetic is shown, and it is never presented as a sourced fact.

**Why 13F is mostly irrelevant here.** It reports only public-equity positions (listed shares, some options and convertibles). Private company stakes, LP interests and pre-IPO rounds never appear. Filers are mostly banks, pensions, mutual funds and hedge funds. A VC firm turns up only after a portfolio company has IPO'd and it still holds the listed stock. Usable uses: (a) confirm that a large family office or crossover fund exists and how big its listed book is; (b) find "crossover" investors. It is not a discovery source for private investing.

**Important structural gap.** US **family offices are excluded from the Advisers Act** under the Family Office Rule 202(a)(11)(G)-1 (adopted 2011). A qualifying single family office does not register and files no Form ADV, so it is invisible in IAPD. Sources: https://akingump.com/communicationcenter/newsalertdetail.aspx?pub=2797 , https://www.squirepattonboggs.com/media/jhifbxur/family-office-insights-family-offices-and-the-investment-advisers-act.pdf (accessed 2026-10-11). Only 76 RIAs have "FAMILY OFFICE" in their name **[computed]**, and those are mostly multi-family-office wealth managers, which is itself a false-positive class.

### 1.6 Which source proves what (verification matrix)

| Claim to verify | Strong free evidence | Weaker evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Entity is a real professional investor (VC/PE manager) | SEC ADV (ERA with 2.B(1) VC exemption, or Schedule D fund type VC/PE); ESMA EuVECA/AIFM; CNB/NBS licence; EIF list; full (not associate) membership of a VC association | Own website; OpenVC/Crunchbase profile |
| It is *still active* | Form D for a new fund in the last 3 years; ADV amendment in the last 12 months; deal press releases in the last 24 months | Website still online |
| It invested in company X, and when | Investee's register filing: UK CS01/SH01/PSC; CZ/SK OR společník/spoločník entry date; SEC Form D related persons (fund level); press release of the round | Investor portfolio page (dated via Wayback) |
| Fund size / AUM | Form D total offering amount and amount sold (per fund); ADV gross asset value per private fund; RAUM (RIAs only); EIF/NDF commitment press releases | Self-reported "AUM" on website/LinkedIn |
| Ticket size | Round amount ÷ number of investors from press releases; Form D minimum investment (LP-side, not ticket) | OpenVC/website "check size" (self-reported) |
| Sector focus | ADV Part 2 brochure; EIF "main sector"; portfolio-company NACE/SIC codes | Website marketing text |

### 2.3 Expected reliability per field

| Field | Expected reliability | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Existence / "is a real investor"** | **High** (≥90% precision) when backed by a regulator row (SEC ADV, ESMA, CNB/NBS) or EIF selection; **Medium** for association-only or website-only records | Regulator rows are legally filed. Association lists mix in service providers (CVCA: 57 of 84 are associates; SLOVCA: 31 associates vs 12 ordinary). Websites outlive funds |
| **Still active** | **Medium.** Decays ~10–20%/yr [estimate] | NVCA: US VC firms fell 3,054 → 2,984 and active investors fell from 25,969 (2021) to 13,634 (2025). Firms that stopped raising (e.g. Foundry) keep websites and registrations for years |
| **Sector focus** | **Medium** | Self-declared marketing categories ("generalist", "tech") are coarse. EIF "main sector" and ADV brochures are better. Inferring from the portfolio (NACE/SIC of investees) is the most honest, but noisy |
| **Stage / typical ticket** | **Low–Medium.** Missing for an estimated 40–60% of records [estimate] | Rarely published by PE/FOs. Round-size ÷ co-investor count gives only a rough per-investor ticket. OpenVC check sizes are self-reported. Form D "minimum investment" is the **LP** minimum, not the ticket |
| **AUM / fund size** | **Low–Medium** | Mixed definitions: regulatory AUM vs. fund commitments vs. gross asset value vs. "assets advised". Often stale (one-off press release at close) or cumulative ("€1bn raised across 5 funds"). Self-reported numbers drift (e.g. Social Capital's own "$2.147bn managed" in its letter). Form D and ADV give per-vehicle numbers that must be aggregated to manager level. ERAs report gross assets only. Family offices almost never disclose AUM |
| **Provenance / source URL** | **High** if recorded at ingest | Under our control. Store URL + access date + source type for each field |

## 3. Typical failure modes and false positives (with real examples)

| # | Failure mode | Real example (evidence) | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vehicle explosion: SPVs, series and co-invest LPs counted as "investors"** | In the last 4 quarters of Form D, **6,609 of 10,248 (64%) distinct "Venture Capital Fund" issuers are series/SPV/co-invest vehicles**, e.g. "NI-0617 Fund III, a series of Roll Up Vehicles, LP", "CA-0223 Fund I, a series of Capital Factory SPVs, LP", "Parkway Co-Investment 12 (Figure Series C) QP LP" **[computed]**. GLEIF has 201,591 active FUND LEIs | Model **Manager → Fund → Vehicle**. Roll vehicles up to the manager via Form D related persons, the ADV Schedule D fund list or GLEIF Level 2 parents. Only managers (and direct investors) become DB records |
| 2 | **Fund vs manager vs compartment duplication** | The EIF list has "Lighthouse Seed Fund" and "Lighthouse Seed Fund (Prague)" (both Lighthouse Ventures GP s.r.o.), and "Nation 1 Fund" and "Nation 1 Fund (Prague)" (both Smart Management VC s.r.o.). There are ~1,097 fund rows but ~696 managers **[computed]** | De-dup on manager legal entity (IČO / company number / LEI / CRD), not on fund name |
| 3 | **Service providers in association member lists** (law firms, M&A advisers, banks, auditors) | CVCA: 57 of 84 members are associates (Clifford Chance, White & Case, Citibank Europe, Deloitte, KPMG). SLOVCA associates include M&A advisers Danube Capital Advisors and HILLBRIDGES, the bank ČSOB, and 11 law firms. BVCA: >200 professional-services members | Take only "full/ordinary" members. Run a negative keyword and NACE filter (69.10 legal, 69.20 accounting, 70.22 consulting, 64.19 banks) |
| 4 | **Regulator lists that mix asset classes** | ESMA AIFM register, Slovakia: HB Reavis Investment Management (real estate), Tatra Asset Management (bank's retail AM), IAD Investments (retail funds) next to Sandberg Capital (PE) **[computed]**. CZ has 75 authorised AIFMs, mostly not PE/VC | Use the regulator as an *existence* proof only. Classify type from fund-level data (ADV fund type, EIF sector, portfolio) |
| 5 | **Crowdfunding platforms ranked as "top investors"** | Crowdcube topped Crunchbase's list of Europe's most active seed investors in 2022 (https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/europe-leading-2022-investors-crowdcube-antler); Seedrs was named the "most active UK investor" (Beauhurst 2019). ESMA lists 261 licensed crowdfunding service providers | Negative list = ESMA ECSP register + known platforms. Tag them as `platform`, not `investor` |
| 6 | **Accelerators and studios with tiny standard tickets** | Antler appears beside Crowdcube in the same Crunchbase 2022 "most active" list | Keep them, but type `accelerator` with ticket bucket < €150k. Never let them dominate "most active" rankings |
| 7 | **Dead or wind-down managers that still look alive** | Foundry Group (≈$3.5bn AUM) announced in Jan 2024 that its 2022 fund is its last (https://www.sethlevine.com/archives/2024/01/endings-and-beginnings.html). | 8 | **Announced but never launched (vapour funds)** | The Czech **Národní inovační fond** (planned state fund-of-funds with EIF, €40m) was cancelled before investing (https://www.e15.cz/byznys/finance-a-bankovnictvi/vlada-uz-nechce-miliardovy-investicni-fond-pro-start-upy-1344277) | Require a first-close or a first-investment proof, not an announcement |

Do not attribute old deals to the wrong successor |
| 10 | **VC turned family office / holding company** | Social Capital stopped raising outside capital in 2018 and became a "technology holding company" investing mostly the founder's money (https://techcrunch.com/2018/09/20/social-capital-will-no-longer-raise-outside-capital/embed/) | Type changes over time: store `type_history`. AUM claims after conversion are self-reported |
| 11 | **Name collisions** | **"CVCA"** = Czech PE & VC Association / Croatian PE & VC Association (cvca.hr) / Canadian VC & PE Association. **"Národní rozvojový fond"** (CZ, infrastructure SICAV) vs **"Národný rozvojový fond II"** (SK, SIH-managed) vs **"Národný holdingový fond"** (SK): web searches in this research mixed them up repeatedly. "Sequoia" also collides with unrelated funds | Match only on registry IDs (IČO, company number, LEI, SEC CRD/CIK) plus country. Use names for display only |
| 12 | **State agencies, development banks and holdings counted as private investors** | SIH (100% owned by SZRB) and Národný holdingový fond appear as SLOVCA members and investors | Type them `public/DFI` or `fund-of-funds`. | 17 | **ToS-restricted sources leaking into the DB** | Crunchbase ToS bans scraping and storing "any significant portion". Dealroom: "don't scrape". OpenCorporates free use excludes corporations. Forbes bans databases and scraping | Use them only for manual lead discovery. Every stored fact must have a free/open primary source behind it |

## 4. Five most useful free sources (ranked)

1. **SEC Form ADV bulk files (IAPD)**: free monthly CSV of ~24,000 advisers with a VC-exemption flag and VC/PE fund flags. It also covers many non-US managers with US LPs. This is the best "real investor" proof.
2. **SEC Form D data sets**: free quarterly TSV. Gives fund-level size, vintage (first sale), and manager linkage through related persons. Needs aggressive SPV filtering (64% of VC issuers are vehicles).
3. **ESMA registers (EuVECA + AIFM, Solr JSON)**: free EU-wide existence proof. 448 active EuVECA and 3,135 active AIFMs; filter AIFMs by asset class.
4. **Company registers with shareholder data**: UK Companies House (free daily PSC snapshot, API, CS01/SH01), CZ justice.cz + ARES, SK ORSR. These prove "invested in X, since date Y".
5. **EIF portfolio PDF + national VC associations' full-member lists + national DFIs (SIH, NRB/NRI)**: curated, due-diligenced lists of real managers, strongest for CEE. The caveats are staleness (EIF list dated 2022) and associate members, which must be excluded.

Not freely usable (lead discovery only): Crunchbase (except CC-BY Basic/2013 Snapshot), Dealroom, PitchBook, Preqin, Finstat API, OpenCorporates (for commercial users), Forbes (as a data feed).

### Reproducibility notes for computed figures

- SEC ERA file: `https://www.sec.gov/files/investment/data/other/information-about-registered-investment-advisers-exempt-reporting-advisers/ia100226-exempt.zip`. The counts use columns `2B(1)`, `Any VC Funds`, `Any PE Funds`, `Main Office Country` and `Total Gross Assets of Private Funds`. The sum of gross assets of VC-exempt ERAs is about $1.28tn; it is not used in the estimates because of fund double counting.
- SEC RIA file: `.../ia100226.zip` (inner file `ia09012026.csv`).
- Form D: `https://www.sec.gov/files/structureddata/data/form-d-data-sets/2025q4_d.zip`, `2026q1_d.zip`; `https://www.sec.gov/files/datastandardsinnovation/data/form-d-data-sets/2026q2_d.zip`, `2026q3_d.zip`. Tables OFFERING (INVESTMENTFUNDTYPE), ISSUERS (ENTITYNAME, IS_PRIMARYISSUER_FLAG=YES). SPV regex: `a series of|series [0-9]|\bSPV\b|co-invest|coinvest`.
- ESMA: `https://registers.esma.europa.eu/solr/esma_registers_upreg/select?q=ae_entityTypeLabel:evc&rows=0&facet=true&facet.field=ae_homeMemberState&facet.field=ae_status&wt=json`.
- GLEIF: `https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records?filter[entity.category]=FUND&filter[entity.status]=ACTIVE&page[size]=1`.
- 13F: `grep -c '^13F-HR ' form.idx` for 2026 QTR3.
- EIF: `pdftotext -layout` of the EIF PDF. Rows containing "Signed" were counted; the manager count is approximate because of the column parse.
- EBAN: `pdftotext` of Stats-Compendium-2024.pdf, Figure 8 (country table) and the summary text.
